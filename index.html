How the System Works Under the Hood

Built using pure Android SDK APIs and bleeding-edge Jetpack Compose patterns:

1. The Invisible Context Hook

OriginFlowAccessibilityService.kt

Android’s AccessibilityService acts as an intelligent sensor. Whenever you highlight text or long-press an element in any third-party app, the system fires a TYPE_VIEW_TEXT_SELECTION_CHANGED event.

Our service extracts the text content and uses node.getBoundsInScreen(rect) to compute the exact screen coordinate (x, y) where your finger touched.

2. Radial Intent-Wheel with Fitts's Law

IntentWheelOverlay.kt

Traditional rectangular menus force your thumb to travel uneven distances and often block the text you were reading.

By arranging our 4 primary actions in a radial orbit (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°), every action is exactly 95 pixels away from your touch anchor. This leverages Fitts's Law for lightning-fast muscle memory, accompanied by spring-physics entrance transitions.

3. Floating Multi-App Workspace Stack

FloatingWorkspaceCard.kt

Rendered via WindowManager.LayoutParams.TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY, this persistent card stays visible even as you change apps.

It features two distinct states: an expanded card for reviewing summaries, schedules, and code extracts, and a collapsed bubble puck with a live item counter that can be dragged to any corner of your screen.

4. The Compose-in-Service Bridge

OverlayLifecycleOwner.kt

By default, Jetpack Compose crashes inside an Android Service with an IllegalStateException: ViewTreeLifecycleOwner not found because Services do not have Activity windows.

Our custom OverlayLifecycleOwner implements LifecycleOwner, SavedStateRegistryOwner, and ViewModelStoreOwner, injecting them directly into the root view tree so modern declarative Compose runs flawlessly in system overlays.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this spy on my banking apps or passwords?

No. Android’s Accessibility framework strictly disables node content reporting for password fields and secure window inputs. Origin-Flow only receives text that you explicitly highlight or select.

Why does Origin-Flow need "Display over other apps"?

This Android permission (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) allows the radial wheel and workspace bubble to appear seamlessly above any running app without having to switch foreground windows.

Will this drain my device's battery?

No. Unlike apps that continuously poll the screen, Origin-Flow is completely event-driven. The service remains idle until an actual text selection or long-press event is dispatched by the OS.

Can I move or close the floating workspace?

Yes! You can drag the workspace anywhere on your display, collapse it into a tiny floating bubble puck, or swipe it closed at any time.
